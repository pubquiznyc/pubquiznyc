Weekly Recap for 09/25/2013

Sometimes the weekly recap happens on a Monday, and that's ok. If delays in getting the recap posted are any indication of the fun had at last Wednesday's quiz, then I'd say we all had 5 days worth of fun. Following that logic, if we don't post a recap, then the fun never ends.

Speaking of never-ending fun, we had some epic birthday chugging between Ian and Mark throughout the evening as teams competed for some pretty great swag from Dyckman Beer Company. Clearly there was one guy in the chug-offs that is trying to corner the market on Dyckman Beer merch, but hopefully some other players got to grab some glassware at the end of the night.

Co-hosting and calling the shots with yours truly was Rear Admiral Jen, your cruise director on this ship of fools as we sailed off into the sunset. Fun fact: in the Royal Netherlands Navy, a rear admiral is known as schout-bij-nacht, which literally translates to "supervisor during the night". She ruled the night with rounds about dissenting opinions in IMDB reviews of great movies, library disappointments, and something called "That's What She Said." Meanwhile, I mailed it in with a this day in history round and a dastardly common bond.

The Wheels on the Bus Go Suck It Trebek won for the night and the month. I could have sworn there were a lot of other teams playing this month, but perhaps not. Well, at least they take good care of our beloved Quizmasters' Cup.

Thanks for showing up and being part of the 5 days of fun. See you all in a couple days when we kick off Octoberfest at the IRC.

Evil Mr. Phil
