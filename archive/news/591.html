Merry Christmas!

All of us here at the Indian Road Café Pub Quiz would like to wish you a Merry Christmas! Unless of course you don't want to be wished a Merry Christmas, in which case we take it back. We can't however take back our gift to you. It's too late for that. Don't worry. We're sure this gift is something you've wanted for a very long time.

It's not a Hoover. Guess again.

No. It's really not a Hoover.

Give up?

Our gift to you on this festive day, is the gift of not having to come to the quiz. That's right! There's no quiz today. We spent all week shopping to find this perfect gift and this is it. And the best thing about it is that you can keep it while also giving it away to someone else! It's just like... well... a contagious disease. Enjoy it. We know we will.

We hope you all have a wonderful week off, and we'll see you bright and early next week on New Year's Day for our Most Hungover Quiz Ever!

Happy Holidays, and I hope that you give or get the Hoover you've always wanted.
