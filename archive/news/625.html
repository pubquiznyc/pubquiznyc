Weekly Recap for 03/26/2014

Another quiz month over, another one about to begin. I know we're all looking forward to the first quiz of April, but first let's take a moment to remember all that happened last week.

First of all, we were honored to have Naomi at the mic guest hosting for the night. Because we had a Canadian-import hosting, we were required to provide at least 40% Canadian content in the quiz. Most of this was in the form of cheesy Canadian pop music at halftime.

For sponsorship, we had Joe from Bellhaven, who spent most of his night trying to park a car in Inwood, but still arrived in time to bring us shirts and pint glasses for prizes.

There were some fantastic trivia rounds on the Good, the Bad, and the Ugly. Also, there were Wizards (by request), Canadian Hosts (by Can-con regulation), and a Common Bond about McDonald's (by IRCPQ tradition). Coming in first place for the night was Trivia De Havilland, but we also awarded our monthly prize. Four weeks of trivia were not enough to determine the top team, and we had to resort to a tie-breaker. It was The Steel Toed Ruby Slippers squeaking past University of Inwood's Re-Accreditation by the slightest of margins to earn their month with our beloved Quizmasters' Cup. Despite losing yet another tie-breaker I think U of I has earned their accreditation.

Now, it's time to think about the first quiz of April. Until then, I think I'll study up on the Canadian Walk of Fame stars. See you next week!

Evil Mr. Phil
