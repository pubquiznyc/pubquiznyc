Weekly Recap for 04/17/2013 and Pajama Jammy Jam Announcement

With rounds on science, products ending in O, and Arrested Development, Wednesday was another triumphant pub quiz. How can we possibly keep up this streak of favorable recaps every single week? When will it ever end? Surely every week’s pub quiz can’t be so fantastic. Eventually there’s bound to be a quiz so poorly conceived and absurd that it will utterly fail. Nah, we’d never let that happen.

...

Next Wednesday, April 24th, we’re proud to present our first ever IRCPQ Pajama Party Night!

The idea was born in the early hours of the morning on April 13th. Dr. Jordan and I were laying in bed, staring up at the ceiling, when suddenly we each turned to the other in unison. Our timing was so perfectly synced that it could have been scripted (or even just made up completely for the sake of an absurd blog post). We turned to each other and at the same time said "I hate pants." It wasn’t the first time we’d declared our distaste for those constrictive lower-body coverings, but this time we had a plan. "Let’s just stay in our pajamas!" we said to each other. And, that’s exactly what we’ve done. Dr. Jordan and myself will be showing up in our pajamas for a Pajama Jammy Jam, and we’d like you all to do the same. In fact, we’ll be awarding a point to every team whose members all show up in pajamas.

Don’t forget that this week is also the last quiz for the month of April and we’ll be awarding our coveted prize pack to the team with the top monthly score. Show up to go for the win. Show up to cheer for another team. Show up in pajamas. Mostly, just show up.

Evil Mr. Phil
