Hint of the Week for November 1st, 2017

Before the hint of the week, we have some announcements. I know you're anxious for the hint, but please bear with me.

Announcement 1

There will be no Halloween costume competition at the quiz tonight. Halloween was yesterday, and Halloween weekend has passed. You can wear a costume if you want - we won't judge you.

Announcement 2

I've heard rumors that last week some logistical changes to our monthly food drive were announced. I don't know what the changes are, but I'm excited to find out. In the meantime, there will be no food drive this evening.

Announcement 3

Later this month I, The Evil Mr. Phil, will be celebrating 40 Years of Evilness, and I'd love to have my pub quiz family there to celebrate.

To commemorate this milestone we will be having a private screening of the Hal Needham classic Rad at Nitehawk Cinemas (in Brooklyn). Our very own Dr. Jordan will be providing AV support. Please RSVP as soon as possible, as seating is limited.

Hint of the Week

I heard there’s an Institute for Mutant Education and Outreach right in Westchester County. I wonder who started it.

See you tonight!
