Weekly Recap for 04/10/2013

Thanks to everyone who braved the thunderstorms and showed up to a very special mid-April edition of the IRCPQ. Hosting with all the lightning flashing in the window behind us was a lot like being in a video for a heavy metal song - a heavy metal song whose lyrics are about world capitals, presidents, and baseball.

With all the birthdays and goings away we had quite a bit of beer to give away lat night. I hope we sent Screamin' Matt off with a bigger Thursday morning hangover than usual. I trust that IRCPQ franchises will be popping up all over Ohio in the next few years. Happy birthdays to Noah, Erin, Brian, and Jason, and thanks for helping us give away a record-setting 21 cans of Genesee Cream Ale.

The Angry Dolphins seemed placated by winning for the night, though Trivialis came very close to holding onto their halftime lead. All in all, there were some solid scores last night. There were also some less solid scores.

The word epic gets thrown around a lot these days, and so I won't hesitate to also throw it around. Last night was an epic pub quiz. We can't wait to top it next week. See you then!

Evil Mr. Phil
