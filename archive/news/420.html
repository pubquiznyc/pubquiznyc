Weekly Recap for 11/14/2012

I wanted to do an audio recap this week, but after hearing what my voice sounds like on Wednesday I thought better of it. Hearing the poor quality of my voice did raise an interesting question: Why do you all keep coming back week after week? Certainly it wasn't for the trivia rounds on cartoon rabbits, James Bond movies, child actors, or elections, though they were pretty good this week. Certainly it wasn't for the Pint on Us toss up questions or the beer chugging, though they'd never been better. It's to take a shot at winning the Quizmasters' Cup, isn't it? I thought so.

Let me take a moment to remind you that here at the Indian Road Café Pub Quiz, we don't take vacations. Ever. There will be a quiz next week on Thanksgiving Eve. This could be a critical week that helps your team win it all for the month, so I encourage you to get a team there whatever it takes. In order to stick around long enough to host the quiz, I'll be traveling on a midnight train to go see my family.

They don't live amidst snow-capped mountains anymore, but my brother and I still wear powder blue leisure suits every chance we get.

Oh, and did I mention that OJ the Imposter will be there on Thanksgiving Eve to astound us with all the new trivia he's been collecting? See you Wednesday!

Evil Mr. Phil
