Weekly Recap for 12/12/2012

If there's one thing the regulars at the IRCPQ crave, it's abuse. Why else would everyone keep coming back week after week. Among this Wednesday's abuses were Teenage Lobotomies, transvaginal wands, and of course opera. Not to mention, we had one of the most abusive quizmasters ever when Nurse Ratched took her first turn at hosting the quiz.

Nurse Ratched, who looked a lot like our usual trivia night bartender, did a fantastic job her first time hosting. After I mailed it in with a round called Last Year's Christmas Movie Questions, she reminded us of all the great people we lost this year with her 2012 In Memoriam round. Next I fulfilled my annual opera requirement with an opera round that actually had a couple actual opera questions about actual opera.

At halftime, our very own OJ the Impostor showed up to announce the winner of our essay contest. Congratulations to Jen, I hope you know what you're getting yourself into. If you want to know what a winning essay looks like, check it out here.

That frivolity didn't last long, because Nurse Ratched brought us all back to the quiz with a painstakingly crafted round 4. Even though I warned her from my own experience that trying to write a limerick round was a fool's errand, she went for it anyway. Not only that, but she had the audacity to knock it out of the park. Some people. Luckily she foisted a Dune round on you guys at the end of the night, so I didn't go home feeling too embarrassed about my poorly written rounds.

Overall, it was a pretty great night, and it was made even better by the very-nearly-triumphant return of the Bunny Slippers. However, at the end of the night it was U of I winning again and building on their lead for the month. Luckily for the rest of you, they'll be losing half their team to holiday parties next week. Make the best of it!

The holidays are indeed upon us, and I won't be at the quiz until January. We already have hosts lined up for the rest of December, but if anyone would like to write weekly recaps we'd be happy to post them to this very website. Feel free to volunteer in the comments, or just email them after the quiz to IndianRoadCafePubQuiz@gmail.com.
