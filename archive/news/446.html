Weekly Recap for 01/09/2013 (Live Blogged Edition)

A DEAFENING BUZZ FROM PA SPEAKERS BLASTS US ALL IN OUR EARHOLES! OH, THE AURAL CARNAGE!

7:55 I'm off duty. They can fix their AV problems themselves.
7:56 Oh, hello dear reader. Welcome to yet another "live blogged" edition of the Indian Road Café Pub Quiz. I have the night off and will be playing with The Evil Mr. Phil and His All Girl Band. We have an all-Jordan pub quiz tonight. So, without further commentary, let the Jordaning begin!

8:11 I got busy eating a plate of the Bobotie and haven't really been paying attention to the pub quiz. It sounds like Dr. Jordan is doing a Name That Director round. The important thing is, though, that the Bobotie is really good. I am seriously enjoying it.
8:15 Done with the directors. Now we may not have gotten all of the correct, but I do believe all the directors were men. I wonder why women never want to direct movies.
8:18 Other Jordan is at the mic with a "ZZ" round. hooray for word play! So many Zs!

8:32 Dr. Jordan has the answers and results for the directors round. Nothing much to say here - our team didn't get many right.
8:34 Round 3 is all about neighborhoods. The way I see the city this is a true-false round since everything is either Inwood or not.

Naomi Major Major just dropped my pen.

8:46 OJ has answers for the Zed Zed round. (Did I mention there's a Canadian on our team?) Lots of 9/10s. There's going to be a chug-off. We have six chuggers in position. And the chugging has begun! Now it's over! (So much excitement.) Speaking of Canadians, a guy in a denim jacket just won the chug off! He's just won himself a 473ml glass with the logo of the IRC emblazoned on the side.

halftime

9:11 Dr. Jordan has neighborhood answers. He just incurred some hatred for not accepting Wall St. as another name for the Financial District. What a "hahn"! Three 9/10s for that round, and it's an all-girl chug-off. Jordan is beside himself. Or, maybe he's just beside Other Jordan. Who can tell?
9:16 OJ has the halftime scores and another pint on us question. IMSA is our team to beat at the half. And, now a round on PBS!
9:26 Ooooh! We at the IRCPQ just unlocked the "Downton Abbey Questions For Two Consecutive Weeks" badge! By the way, we have excellent Foursquare specials at the IRCPQ. Don't forget to check in.
9:30 Round 5! The Penultimate round! Dr. Jordan is doing a round on the Bill of Rights. We sure love Dr. Jordan.
9:35 I'm thinking of writing a pub quiz bill of rights. We already have a few entries in our bylaws that might fit: Chuggers shall not be made to eat cake before chugging, Chuggers shall not be made to chug green beer on St. Patrick's Day, Danielle shall receive a half can of chugging beer if there is a half can of chugging beer left over at the end of the night,. And now we can add, "Dr. Jordan shall never again ask us to recall the amendments of the Bill of Rights by number".
9:40 OJ's back. We love OJ, too. He's got another all girl chug-off on his hands. Ladies night? Of course, every night is ladies night at the IRCPQ.
9:51 Common Bond time!

working on common bond

9:56 Dr. Jordan's got the Bill of Rights answers. This is a good time to order more beer.
10:00 Now we wait for Common Bond and end of night results. Who will walk away victorious, and who will just walk away?
10:15 OJ has results. I'm so excited! Hey, the Common Bond is BOWLS! University of Inwood has a perfect score and wins a pint glass!
10:17 Final scores! A close game, but IMSA gets the $25 gift certificate. Looks like the monthly scores are getting close as well.

They're playing the outro... and... we're... out. Good night!

Evil Mr. Phil
