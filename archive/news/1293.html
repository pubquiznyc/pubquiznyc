Weekly Recap for June 17, 2020

We haven't had a recap in a while. It is possible that so many hosts were BTOB that the memories of whatever they'd done during Quiz, had been drowned in a soup of high quality potables and high fat edibles of various sorts.

Uptown Phil Brown stepped into the breach, and conjured up rounds about birbs and two-word phrases and rows. Mary O'Shag asked about firsts and lasts and cars and Pride.

We remain grateful to Professor Lone Star for stabilizing (to the extent this cast of characters can ever be called stable) the show on Streamyard.

Next Wednesday, more Quiz. Join us at 8, and pour your own.

Mary O'Shag
