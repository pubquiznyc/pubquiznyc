Weekly Recap for 09/19/2012

A friendly reminder to all you trivia boffins out there. Our pub quiz happens whether "The Boss" is in town or not. Bruce Springsteen is not "the boss" of the Indian Road Café Pub Quiz.

Not only does the pub quiz always happen, but sometimes it's even good. This week's pub quiz was a good one. Not only were there questions about titular characters, but there was a question about a titular character's namesake.* There was a question about Bruce Springsteen, though he didn't get to choose which one since he's not the boss of us. We really went all out.** And, at the end of the night there was nothing left but a half-can of Genesee. I sincerely feel bad for people that lack proper decision-making skills, and willfully chose to not be at the pub quiz this Wednesday.

Guest host Mary O'Shag had her quizmastering debut, and fortunately for you she went easy on everyone. You have no idea what she's capable of. There were rounds on music videos, subways, stones, voiceover artists, and a common bond on streets of Inwood. A good time was had by all, and Schtikel In Your Whoo-ha won for the night (despite Jon Wunder's excessive birthday drinking).

Now, it's time for yo-yos in space...

Or, perhaps you'd prefer Beards in Space.

Or, maybe you'd just like to be cast in a gameshow.

Whatever you do, don't miss next week's pub quiz! See you there.

* - It was a Big Lebowski question.
** - Or, did we go all in?

Evil Mr. Phil
