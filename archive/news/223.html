11/08/2011
Mo-vember

We're a week into November and I'm sure you're wondering how the mustached-men of the IRCPQ are doing. If you don't know what I'm talking about you should check out us.movember.com to find out more about this month long charity event.

I encourage you to support our fellow IRCPQ-ers in their endeavors by contributing at their mo-space pages. I've been told that although contributions of lightly used facial-hair are appreciated, they can't be applied to the participants own growth and won't be tallied on the website.

Dr. Jordan is our soon to be returning from estrangement quizmaster and founder of the IRCPQ. We're still waiting for some pictures of his elusive mustache.

Noah Houghland made the ultimate sacrifice by shaving off a sweet 'stache midway through Halloween week even though it was critical to his costume.

Jordan Laks lacks nothing in his life but a mustache, but he plans to have a perfect life by the end of this month.

If you're participating in this madness and you want your name added to this list, let me know what your mo-space page is and I'll add a link.

Evil Mr. Phil
