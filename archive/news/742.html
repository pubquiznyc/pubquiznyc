May 6th: Cinco de Cano

April was a very exciting month at the IRCPQ, but it's over. I can't promise that May will be as exciting, but I can promise that your team will get bonus points if every member brings a non-perishable food donation to the quiz tonight!

Everyone is encouraged to bring donations of nonperishable, non-glass-packaged foodstuffs for Good Shepherd's food pantry. A single bonus point will be added to your team's score at the end of the night if every member of your team brings an item.

Thanks and/or blame go to our very own Mary O'Shag for organizing this and transporting the donations. She has asked us to ask you to consider spices, non-salt seasonings, and low-sugar snack items. Your support will be greatly appreciated!

Won't you please show Mary your cans tonight.

Evil Mr. Phil
