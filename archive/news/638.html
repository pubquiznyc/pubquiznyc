Weekly Recap for 04/30/2014

Thanks to everyone who braved the April monsoons and skipped playoff hockey to come to the quiz last night. I think you were well rewarded with beer and t-shirts, though maybe not as well rewarded as last week.

OJ the Imposter seems to have returned to his regular hosting schedule and was there last night with rounds on Science, Movie Trailers, and Winnie the Pooh. I had Railroads, First Lines, and The A-Team, all vaguely by request.

We were up against the Rangers/Flyers game last night, and like the NHL refs we tried to keep our officiating to a minimum and let you guys play. Also like the NHL refs, we often failed at that. Trivialis From Concentrate pulled a NY Rangers, and won for the night. University of Inwood Spring Break Rehydration Center won for the month, taking home the cup - something the NY Rangers should learn from. Speaking of cups, the Quizmasters' Cup is getting spiffier looking every month. You winning teams have really outdone yourselves.

I think it was a pretty successful April for the IRCPQ. I can't wait to see what happens in May.

Evil Mr. Phil
