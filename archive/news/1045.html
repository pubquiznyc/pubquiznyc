QUIZ CANCELLED DUE TO VD

QUIZ CANCELLED: February 14, 2018.

When you host a quiz for almost 10 years, it's bound to happen. Because of VD, the Indian Road Café has decided to not have a quiz today. They are running a restaurant after all.

NOW WHAT? To fill the void VD left in your life there are a couple things you can do:
- Take a quiz to see What IRCPQ Quizmaster Are You?
- Form a team and compete at tomorrow's 2018 Beer Olympics!

Whatever you do, don't despair.

QUIZ RETURNING: February 21, 2018.

The quiz will be back to its regularly scheduled time and place next Wednesday. We will spend the off week thinking about VD and wondering how it we let things go this far - so don't expect us to put twice as much work into the following quiz.

Evil Mr. Phil
