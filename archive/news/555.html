Weekly Recap for 10/02/2013

Oktoberfest! Of all the toberfests, Oktoberfest is my favorite.

This Wednesday we kicked off the Indian Road Café's Oktoberfest celebration. There was seemingly no end to all the beer and sausage. Dr. Jordan and I each got completely stuffed with sausage before the quiz started, which is why we were in such good moods. There was so much beer we had to put it into boots. And, we even listened to some tuba music!

Oh, and let us not forget the trivia questions. We covered a wide range of topics such as sausages, Oktoberfest, Germans, Geography "G", "Repairing Bad", and a common bond on hair. At the end of the night the teams proved to be evenly matched - with 9 teams finishing in the top 3. That doesn't sound like proper math, but I assure you it all works out. We had a 3-way tie-breaker to decide our nightly winner, and it was Waffles, Friends, and Work besting Ich Liebe Octoberfest and Pumpkin Flavored Seaman Cumming for the win.

I hope you all had as much fun Oktoberfest-ing as we did, and we'll see you next week for a very special event called Octoberninth.

Oh, and Pumking is back on tap. But, this year I remembered how high in alcohol it is. Not so much when the IRC put Pumking on tap in 2012.

Evil Mr. Phil
