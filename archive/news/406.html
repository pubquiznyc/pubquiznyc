Weekly Recap for 10/17/2012

LL Cool J once said, "Don't call it a recap." I don't know what he meant by that, but the ladies sure loved him. Something else the ladies sure love is The Indian Road Café Pub Quiz.

Last night Jon "Wonder" Wunder kicked the pub quiz off right by channeling OJ the Imposter's love of wordplay and giving us a round called "My Friend Nia". After that I fulfilled our quarterly obligation to do a science round.* And just like every other quarter's science round it had too many questions about scurvy. Before the halftime break, Jon sated the most impatient quizzers with a mini common bond round. It was pretty good, but I was a little sad that there were no hockey players in the set of "rangers".**

After the break we had a deceptively hard round where each answer was a number from 1 to 10 with no repeats. That probably caused a few arguments within the teams. At least, I hope it did. Round 5, the penultimate round, brought more wordplay, with every question or answer containing "drag". Once the common bond round was scored we had determined our nightly winners. Random Chaos somehow acquired half a point***, and slipped past Seaman Debates Cumming to win the $25 gift certificate. Congratulations to Random Chaos, and congratulations to everyone who won candy last night. Remember to have your parents check the candy before you start pigging out.

In other news, I'm going to be climbing the stairs of a 42 story building for charity this evening. The organization is Story by Story, who are raising money to help end domestic violence. My stair climbing on its own can only raise as much money as the change that falls out of my pockets while climbing, so I'd like you to consider making a donation. Don't worry if you don't get your donation in by tonight's event. We'll continue accepting donations for a couple more weeks.

Our very own Mary O'Shag is going to be working first aid tonight, and she has assured me that I will survive it. However, if I don't survive, please consider making a donation in lieu of flowers at my funeral. I trust Mary's abilities, but I'm not sure she knows the extent of my unhealthiness. We'll see.

I hate to be too overly optimistic, but see you next week!

* - It's in the official IRCPQ bylaws.
** - Oh hockey, I pine for you.
*** - It's all very random.
