A Fund-Razor Night

What if you could go back in time?
What if you could return to the past?
What if you could see what The Evil Mr. Phil looked like before he had a beard?
Well, now you can!

On October 23rd, at the end of our regularly scheduled pub quiz, we'll be removing The Evil Mr. Phil's beard. Nay! We'll be letting you remove the beard. If you're one of the top donations of the night, you'll get a chance to take sharp, pointy objects to The Evil Mr. Phil's beard. We will continue the application of sharp objects to the beard until it is no longer there.

Donations all go to the Leukemia & Lymphoma Society, and can be made in person the night of the event or any time leading up to the event via http://crowdrise.com/IRCPQ.

Some people have told me it's crazy to let people take scissors and razors to my face after they've spent the night drinking at a pub quiz. To those people, I say, "This beard goes, or I do!"

Evil Mr. Phil
