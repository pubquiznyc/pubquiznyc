Weekly Recap for 10/31/2012 (Halloween Spooktacular Edition)

As Tim Curry says, "Anything can happen on Halloween." And as always, Tim Curry is right. This week's pub quiz was one for the record books.

Halloween falling on a Wednesday, special guest hosts Fozzie and George, and a hurricane making the IRCPQ the only place for Inwoodites to go, all combined to make this the most crowded pub quiz we've ever had. There were lots of fantastic costumes and everyone was in great spirits despite many being forced to stand. We also had Tony in the "DJ booth", providing thematic music throughout the night. Oh, and let's not forget the fortune teller at the front of the room.

We at the IRCPQ were more than happy to leave our quiz in the capable hands of Fozzie and George. There were lots of logistics to deal with and a mob of people, and that kind of thing really gets in the way of drinking on a Wednesday night. They had rounds on mythical monsters, murder weapons, Buffy, and so much more.* Although I can't remember all the rounds, I do remember that they all embodied the true spirit of Halloween where anything can happen.

Right, Tim Curry?

Because of the overwhelming crowds, we were unable to have a costumed chug-off, but we fell back on the original plan of awarding a prize to the best Frida Kahlo of the night. Your 2012 winner of best Frida was Megan from Night of the Living Bunny Slippers.** By the end of the night, we had a clear winner, and it was our new regulars The Red Shoes. Not only did they win for the night, but they also gained enough points to surpass Seaman Debates Cumming for top score of the month of October. Quite the accomplishment!***

Congratulations to the Red Shoes, who took home our beloved Quizmasters' Cup in its Stewie Griffin costume. They'll also get a cocktail named in their honor next week and tickets from All Shows. I don't currently have contact info for the Red Shoes, so if anyone on the Red Shoes is reading this and wants to put in a request for a cocktail ingredient and/or get the ball rolling on the All Shows tickets, please contact us via facebook. Or, we'll just talk to you next week.

* - Freed from my usual quizmasterly duties, I took the opportunity to consume of a few tasty adult beverages.
** - I believe the prize was a new razor blade, but I'm not sure.
*** - Once the Red Shoes have been coming for a while, they'll realize that surpassing Seaman and Cumming isn't much of an accomplishment.
