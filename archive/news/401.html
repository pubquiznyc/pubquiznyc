Weekly Recap for 10/03/2012

I've written before about decision-making skills, and today I'd like to again. A key element of a pub quiz is decision making. Often you have to decide between two competing options, because yet again you don't really know the answer and yet again you are forced to guess. There's also deciding on team names and deciding on beers. I can't stress enough how important decision making is, which is why I get so worried when I see people struggling with the simplest of all decisions: whether or not to come to the Indian Road Café Pub Quiz.

I'd like to applaud the wisdom of those who made the right decision this Wednesday and came to the pub quiz rather than watching some presidential debate nonsense. I don't even see how these two events compare. Actually... maybe there are some similarities.

How the IRCPQ Compares to a Presidential Debate

  • Both have difficult questions.
  • Both have participants that misconstrue said questions.
  • Both are riddled with factual errors.
  • Both have highly-skilled, well-prepared, good-looking hosts.
  • If the Pub Quiz ends in a tie, it's settled by having everyone drink beer.
  • If a debate ends in a tie, it's settled by an election - where instead of beer they give you a sticker.

I think the decision is clear...

In quiz news, we held to our promise and avoided US History, opting instead for Canadian History. For those who wish to learn more about this mysterious land to our north, check out http://www.thecanadianencyclopedia.com/. Also featured this week were Sci-Fi, Brit-Lit, Scrabble and "Lice's Adventures in Wonderland". As for the Common Bond round, let's just remember that it all worked out in the end. No hard feelings, right? I guess we'll find out next week, assuming you make the right decision and show up.

By the way, I'm not going to be at the quiz next week. Have fun.

Evil Mr. Phil
