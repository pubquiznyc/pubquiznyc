Hint of the Week for April

In an unprecedented turn of events the IRCPQ has decided to release its weekly Hint of the Week a few days early. Why you might ask? Because*, in an equally unprecedented turn of events and for the first time ever, one of the rounds for this week's upcoming quiz will be based entirely on one single YouTube video. That's right. Ten questions all from the same short video.

We feel that it's only fair to give every team at least 72 hours to study and prepare for the quiz this week, and we also feel that it's only fair that you see the video in question. This is some pretty serious stuff, so you should watch it with someone you love.**

Welcome back. Did you like it?

Good.

We thought you'd like it. We're a little worried, though. Since the next quiz will be the first Wednesday of a new month, we're worried that everyone will choose "Dog-faced Legumes" as their team name. Alas. I guess that's just one more risk we'll be taking in what might become a very risky pub quiz. Seriously, this week's pub quiz is going to be off the hook. I don't remember it ever being on the hook***, but I digress. There are so many amazing things planned that I can hardly contain my excitement. All I can say is "wow", you're not going to want to miss this.
