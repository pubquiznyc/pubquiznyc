Weekly Recap for 09/18/2013

Another raucous night of trivia is in the books, and what do you have to show for it?

Well, if you were at the quiz this week you might have t-shirts, beer, stickers, bottle openers, or temporary tattoos.* Thanks to David from Saranac, we had piles and piles of Sara-nic-nacs to send you home with. Not bad for a Wednesday's night of knowledge work.

As for the questions, we the quizmasters did our best to fulfill all of you the quizzers' wildest quiz fantasies by actually responding to your numerous round requests. We had Photography, Pennsylvania, Manhattan Math, and even a Red Shoes going away round. We're sorry to be losing one red shoe, but perhaps the remaining red shoe(s) will continue to lace 'em up** for the quiz.

Jon Wunder had a birthday to celebrate, and despite his advancing age and current state of inebriation, he and the rest of The Wheels on the Bus Go Suck It Trebek finished with a win for the night. Now remember, next week is the final week of the month. It looks like they've got a solid lead yet again - are we going to have to put out some kind of bounty? Perhaps free concert tickets to whatever team can topple Suck It Trebek for the month? I think we'll do exactly that.

So, come back next Wednesday to claim the prize!

* - I still haven't been able to wash off any of these "temporary" tattoos.
** - Get pumped for the quiz? Sneaker 'round for a quiz? Be suede by the lure of the quiz? Loaf around Inwood for the quiz?
