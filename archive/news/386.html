Weekly Recap for the Nerd Night Mixer

As much as I dislike calling people names, you all really are a bunch of nerds. The Nerd Night Mixer was a blast, and I'd like to thank everyone who showed up and was willing to reveal their true nerdiness to people they may have just met. Also, I'd like to thank the NNM's benefactor, Minty-Minter, for embracing the event and finding us such fantastically nerdy prizes. A special thank you to the nerdiest bartender in the room,* Rachel, for helping us make all this happen and letting us have a Dr. Horrible Singalong Afterglow. Finally, thanks to the staff at IRC for putting up with our shenanigans, we really don't deserve you guys.**

Much happened on Wednesday, but alas I must go and seek out new trivia in the wilds of upstate New York. Feel free to add comments below to tell us how you liked it.

* - Depending on the room.
** - We certainly don't deserve to be put on your shoulders.
