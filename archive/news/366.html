Weekly Recap for 07/18/2012

This Wednesday's pub quiz was intense. And not just the normal kind of intense. This quiz was intensely intense. On a scale from 1 to 10, where 10 is the most intense thing ever, the scale immediately exploded into a huge fireball, leaving nothing behind to help us measure intensity ever again. Now, you may be wondering what happened to raise the intensity to such unheard-of levels. Well... intensity happened!

Substitute quizmaster, Jenn-ay, brought the intensity from the very beginning with a round all about Jennies that caused several people to completely lose their minds and just start yelling answers. Dr. Jordan's question-asking was so intense that he had to take several mid-round breaks just to keep everything under control (though, none of us minded waiting). There was an intense 14-way tie-breaking chug-off before we even got to the second half of the quiz.

I honestly don't remember what happened in the second half of the quiz. I can see on the score sheet that we had 18 teams combining for 762 total points over the 6 rounds of trivia, and that Random Chaos won for the night by just a single point. But, that's it. If anyone can recall anything else, please comment at the bottom so we can piece this thing together Hangover-style.

Speaking of hangovers, it was Brenda's birthday on Wednesday. Happy birthday, Brenda. We hope you enjoyed the hangover we gave you.
