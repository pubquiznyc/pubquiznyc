Weekly Recap for 11/06/2013

I'd like to apologize for spending all of round five yelling at everyone last week. All of us here at the Indian Road Café Pub Quiz have been under a lot of stress with the holidays upon us. I know it's no excuse for shouting, but I want you all to understand that you've done nothing wrong. It's not your fault... It's not your fault... It's not your fault... It's not your fault... IT'S NOT YOUR FAULT!

NUTS! I did it again. I'm sorry. In fact, I think you all did an admirable job answering questions on Wednesday. In my book, you're all winners.

My book is of the fantasy genre, though. In the real book, there was only one winner. Duke Silver and the Pawnee Goddesses edged out Oh Magaw Becky Look at Her Butt! in a tie-breaker at the end of the night to earn the title of "winners".

Anyway, the holidays are coming. Which is to say, we're all a little overworked. Which is to say, some of us may be missing a few Wednesdays in the next couple months. Which is to say, the season of guest quizmasters is here!

We ask this every year, but here goes. Have you ever thought about being a quizmaster? That's right, we're looking for volunteers to be guest quizmasters. Imagine. You as the quizmaster.

If you're interested, please talk to a quizmaster on Wednesday night and let them know what weeks you are available. All are welcome to guest host - even girls!
