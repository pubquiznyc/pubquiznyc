Weekly Recap for 09/11/2013

I can't believe we're still getting such warm weather this far into September. And, was it ever hot this Wednesday.

How hot was it?

It was so hot that...

Luckily we had plenty of beer to beat the heat. And, although some of the rounds were painfully difficult, nobody got seriously hurt. There were rounds on cocktails, best actresses, "bad" things, US History, puppets, and a fiendish common bond round about shoes. Four of the rounds even had actual questions and not just lists of things!

We also celebrated Kevin's birthday by making him chug a lot of beer with no hope of winning any prizes. I'm pretty sure that's exactly what he wanted. There were lots of great scores, but The Red Shoes held onto their impressive halftime lead and ended with the win.

Now, I'm going to try to keep my keyboard from catching on fire. See you next week when hopefully things will cool down a bit. Maybe we'll do a round on the weather. Maybe not.
