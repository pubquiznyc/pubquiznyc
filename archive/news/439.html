Weekly Recap for 12/26/2012

December 26th was a beautiful night of rain and beer and trivia for the beautiful people at the Indian Road Cafe Pub Quiz.

Did you know that December 26th is Chairman Mao's birthday? Did you know that December 26th is also Boxing Day? Well, Major Naomi Major sure did. Her pint on us before round one was about Mao's birthday. Her first round was about Boxing Day? You know who knows about Mao and Boxing? Woman and Husband, that's who. Woman won the pint on us and husband won the first chug. Maybe he didn't. I don't know. But, they did win the first round. The second too, the one about Christmas in New York.

After the third round, about subways around the world--not the fast food chain--Woman and Husband held a lead. However, they didn't hold on to it. The fourth round about greed, the fifth round about communism and the sixth round about nine questions that were seeming unrelated but all about hotels elicited a rousing come from behind victory for The Corner.

The Corner is a great book by David Simon. At first, I thought they had named themselves after that book. Then I realized they just named themselves for where they were sitting, which is pretty lame. What if Random Chaos renamed themselves At the Bar? What if Woman and Husband went with The Round Table?

Not cool, right?

Well, we're happy The Corner won. We hope they return with a new name to defend their crown. The same goes for all the new teams who joined and stayed to the end. Come back. We're here every week. Every single week. Even the day after Christmas.

Finally, a big congratulations to Seaman Claus Cumming to Town. They won the IRCPQ Quizmasters Championship Cup and a cocktail in their honor and two tickets to any show of their choice. Treat the cup well and take lots of pictures of it. We all look forward to seeing it.

Next week starts a new month in a new year. Get your team's name ready and stick to those resolutions. I'm talking to you, The Resolutions.

On behalf of the other quizmasters, Happy New Year everybody. Thank you for another wonderful year at the quiz.

OJ the Imposter
