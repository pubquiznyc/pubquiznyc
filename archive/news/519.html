Weekly Recap for 07/03/2013

I hope everyone had a fun and patriotic Fourth of July holiday. I imagine you're all now getting back to the business of writing your pub quiz recaps. I know I am.

It was quite the Wednesday night. We were sponsored by Shmaltz, who gave out some beer and provided us with tickets to their new brewery's grand opening this weekend. There was a buzz and excitement that had been lacking recently. Perhaps it was the free beer, or perhaps it was because the quiz was on the eve of a holiday, but I'd like to think that word finally got out that our very own OJ the Imposter was back under the bright lights of the IRCPQ's Quizmaster Corner.

I don't remember many details about the quiz, but I do remember that everyone loved all of our questions. Also, My Ass is On Fire won for the night. Hopefully they can use their winnings to cool that fiery ass.

Evil Mr. Phil
