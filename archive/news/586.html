Weekly Recap for 12/04/2013

Last week's form-letter recap remains unfinished, and so the conclusion of November is left shrouded in mystery. But, we at the Indian Road Café Pub Quiz are forward looking. We leave November behind and move on to December. December, that strangest month of the pub quiz calendar. That wily beast that has this year given us a three week quiz month. That year-capping month that makes winners out of teams that best overcome the attrition of the holidays and makes losers out of teams that choose family and friends over pub quiz glory. December.

December began in the usual way, with six rounds of obscure trivia, shout-out questions, and beer chugging. We had Dr. Jordan at the helm with guest host Noah (whose quizmaster name I cannot recall). There were rounds on Geography, Science, Oscar winning anagrams, John Williams, Heroes and Villains, and a common bond. Scores were tight at halftime with a tie between University of Inwood and Duke Silver, but by the end of the night the scores were still tight and the tie had not been broken. Tie breaker questions were used, and Duke Silver showed their knowledge of wall lengths to win for the night.

Speaking of Geography, if you had any trouble remembering that Panama's an isthmus, then this song will make sure you never forget it again. (And, if you didn't figure it out from the title, this guy uses words that start with F).

See you next week.

Evil Mr. Phil
