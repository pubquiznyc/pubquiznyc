Weekly Recap for 01/02/2013

We at the Indian Road Café Pub Quiz work hard to make hosting a pub quiz look really, really difficult. Actually, the beer does most of that hard work for us. The difficulties of last night's quiz are thanks to Stone's Vertical Epic Ale and Bronx's latest barrel-aged ale.* Thems were some excellent beers.

Lucky for us, you guys were either too polite or too drunk to get up and leave while we bombarded you with questions on social media, Craigslist, and Google doodles. We righted the ship a bit in the second half with a mash-ups round, a mini common bond, and a full-fledged common bond.

Unfortunately, there was a tie at the end of the night between Random Chaos and Seaman's Resolution: More Cumming. You may not know this, but we quizmasters carefully regulate our alcohol intake so that we're just competent enough to get through 6 rounds of trivia. A tie-breaker means overtime, and overtime means sloppy quizmastering. After incorrectly grading the tie breaker, incorrectly announcing the winner, and incorrectly correcting the incorrect announcement, we gave up and awarded the $25 gift certificate to Seaman's Resolution. I don't know if we got it right, but we're curious to see if Rachel can concoct another Seaman and Cumming cocktail for next month.

* - Oh, it's also thanks to you guys. You're all wonderful. We'd rather be with you people at the IRCPQ than with the finest people in the world.

Evil Mr. Phil
