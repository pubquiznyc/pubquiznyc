Weekly Recap for 07/10/2013

This Wednesday's quiz was... Wednesday's quiz was quite... Wednesday's quiz was fairly...

I'm finding a difficult word or phrase to capture the essence of the quiz this week. How about... Wednesday's quiz was... over by 10:15? Yeah. I'll stick with that. Let's move on.

Beer was disappearing in chug-offs like lobster shirts flying off the shelves at an Old Navy. Anchor sponsored the night and provided beer, shirts, and pins (though, none of it had lobsters on it). There was laughter, there was tears, there were rounds on spies, geography, movie quotes, and sports. There was something for everybody (except lobster lovers).

Last Wednesday's quiz was a hard-fought battle. We had a four-way tie at halftime. Most teams were in it until the bitter end. Dr. Jordan and I were nervous that we'd have to work overtime to break the tie, however Suck it Trebek! eked out a win at the last minute.

Although we're very happy for them, it sure would be nice to see some other teams in the winner's circle. Who among you will step up to sieze the victory crown next week?

Evil Mr. Phil
