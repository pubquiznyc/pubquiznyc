Weekly Recap for 03/12/2014 (Ladies' Night)

Ladies' Night, oh what a night. Thank you to everyone who showed up on Wednesday to make Ladies' Night such a success. We're hoping to get just as much support for all the Mens' Nights we have lined up.

It was great night before it even started because I got to co-host with not one, but two Jordans. If that wasn't enough, we were also sponsored by Genesee, whose prizes of shirts and hats were delivered by yet another Jordan! So much Jordan!

All of the ladies in attendance excelled at our rounds about First Females, Women Athletes, Lady Bits, and Hats. The ladies also excelled at chugging beer. I believe we gave out almost 77% as much beer as on a Mens' Night quiz.

All the excellent question answering and chugging couldn't compare to my favorite moment of the evening. Our very own Scotsman, Robin, read for us his favorite Robbie Burns poem, Johnnie Lad, Cock Up Your Beaver. That, and we finally got a viable design for an IRCPQ T-Shirt:

I believe Joey of The Trouble With Tribbialis drew this for us. At least, she's the one that handed it to me. If it was someone else's work please let me know so that we can send the royalty checks to the right place.

Finally, at the end of the night, it was the predominantly female Steel Toed Ruby Slippers coming in first and winning the gift certificate to the Indian Road Café and Pub Quiz. Congratulations guys, and see you next week!

Evil Mr. Phil
