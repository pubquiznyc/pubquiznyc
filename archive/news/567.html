Weekly Recap for 10/23/2013

We did it. We certainly did it. In a move that contradicted all calendars and lunar cycles, we had our final quiz of the month October a week early. Amazed? Probably. Confused? Don't be. The explanation is simple.

We're having our second ever Mixer Night next week! This will be a night where the points don't count toward your team's monthly total, so you're free to go join another team for the night. What a great opportunity to meet new people. Sort of.

Next week will also be our Halloween party! Show up in a costume and participate in our storied Costume Chug-Off at half time. What a great opportunity to conceal your identity and get away with things you wouldn't ordinarily do since they won't remember what you really look like.

We're so convinced that you're going to have fun attempting to make friends with people you'll never again recognize that WE are forcing everyone who plays next week to join a team of OUR choosing. That's right. We're controlling everything. Don't be scared. Give in to us. You'll love us. Or else!

Now, about this past Wednesday. OJ and I had a smashing time hosting it, and I hope you had a smashing time attending - or at least you got smashed. In an homage to the events to follow the quiz there were rounds on beards, missing things, fundraisers, things with beards in them, and the Common Bond was even "Smooth". How incredibly topical.

Although we tried to wrap up fast to leave time at the end of the night, three teams thought otherwise. At the end of the night we had a three-way between University of Inwood Pagan Club's Halloween Hades House, Suck It Trebek!: 2 Minutes for Roughing, and Waffles, Friends, and Work. So... we gave them tie-breaker questions. And whadya know... they tied after the tie-breaker. So we went with the tie-breaker-breaker rule of first sheet to the table. That went to the University of Inwood who had the foresight to sit at the table closest to the front.

Although they didn't win the tie-breaker for the night, finishing first for the month and winning our coveted Quizmasters' Cup was Waffles, Friends, and Work. They got to take home The Cup, tickets from All Shows, a cocktail named in their honor next week (or maybe the week after), and all the serial commas they can eat. Congratulations guys!

We're looking forward to seeing everybody's amazing costumes next week. It's gonna be great!

There was also this Fund-Razor nonsense at the end of the night, but we'll get to that in a separate post.

Evil Mr. Phil
