Happy New Year!

It's been a long time since we had our last quiz of 2012, and the Indian Road Café Pub Quiz is ready to kick off our first quiz of 2013 with a vengeance. And, why shouldn't we be vengefully kicking things? It's Episode 216!

This isn't meant to be a hint, we here at the IRCPQ just thought you might like to get to know our friend The Number 216 a little better. We're also really excited about our Episode 216, because that's the number of pub quizzes that Malcolm Gladwell said it will take to get it right. It's true.* We've even made a New Year's resolution to improve the quiz: In 2013 we will aim for over 50% accuracy for most of our questions!

You're not going to want to miss the beginning of the 2013 quiz year. We're back to a three quizmaster rotation, there will be a Seaman and Cumming cocktail, we've resolved to ask more accurate questions, and if everything goes according to plan... somebody will drink a beer! But, you'll have to show up to find out who it is.

See you tomorrow and happy New Year!

* - Ah, truth. Isn't it odd that fallible and infallible mean the same thing? Trust me, I'm never wrong.

Evil Mr. Phil
