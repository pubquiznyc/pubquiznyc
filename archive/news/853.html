Hint of the Week for 05/04/2016

If he wasn't bald-headed, would he have starred in The Scalphunters in '68?
If he wasn't nominated Best Supporting Actor in '62, would it have gone to the birds?
If he hadn't died in '94. Would he be 94 today?
And if he became a one-hit wonder in '75, shouldn't you look up a recording of it and imagine the previous lines set to its tune.

While you untangle that one, don't forget this is also the first Wednesday of a new month and Mary O'Shag will be in attendance to collect your cans.

Show us your cans!

Standard rules apply. If you want a bonus point for your team, you'd better show up with the goods.

We're collecting donations for the Good Shepherd food pantry. If everyone on your team complies and brings in a donation, then your team will get a point added to your score at the end of the night.

Low sodium and low sugar foods are particularly helpful and wanted, as are seasonings (plain boiled pasta gets tiring, no matter how hungry you are.) Child-appealing products are great too.
