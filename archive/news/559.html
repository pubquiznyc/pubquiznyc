Weekly Recap for 10/09/2013

With its days numbered and awaiting its scheduled termination in two weeks, a man's beard's thoughts turn inward. It looks inside itself and faces a... well... a face. As was announced at the quiz last night, the beard will be removed at A Fund-Razor Night on October 23rd.

Coincidentally, we'll also be giving away our beloved Quizmasters' Cup that night. That's right. We have once again bent time, and we have declared the 23rd as the final Wednesday of the month. That means the pub quiz on the 30th won't count toward your teams' monthly scores - leaving you free to join other teams. Perhaps "free" to join other teams isn't the right way to put it. We're going to force you to join other teams whether you like it or not. On October 30th, 2013, we invite you to join us for the IRCPQ Halloween Mixer Quiz! We'll be revealing more details about the event as we decide what they are, but one detail we've already decided on is that the night will be awesome.

Speaking of awesome, last night's quiz wasn't far off the mark. We had rounds on all your favorite things: Seinfeld, Rockets, and Heteronyms. Also, some movie mashups, some wordplay, and a common bond round. Everyone was thoroughly impressed - except maybe the kid lurking at the front of the room that told me, "You'd have to be a hobo spending all your days researching at the library to even get half these questions right."*

At halftime it was The Red Shoes and Always the Trivialis, Never the Bride neck and neck for the lead. But, as their name predicted, Trivialis was jilted again as The Red Shoes eked out the win.** Show up next week to see who crushes their hopes and dreams next. Maybe it will be you!

* - Seriously. That's the kind of stuff you hear as a quizmaster.
** - Ahhhhhhhhh, Eke Out! Le freak, c'est chic!
