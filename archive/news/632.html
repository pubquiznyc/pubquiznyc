Literary Pub Quiz

Take your finger out of your nose, and come to tomorrow's Literary Pub Quiz! That's right. We at the Indian Road Café Pub Quiz would like you to come to our first ever Literary Pub Quiz tomorrow night.

As part of World Book Night, we're going to be giving away free books to anyone willing and able to read them. We may also have books as prizes. Who knows, there might even be questions about books. Whatever happens, books will be involved.

As always, show up early to get a table. And, remember to check back tomorrow morning for the Hint of the Week.
