Weekly Recap for 04/24/2013

Who would have thought that our Pajama Jammy Jam would be upstaged by our freshly decorated Quizmasters' Cup? Everyone looked awfully cozy in their PJs, but That's What Grandma Said at the Orgy (aka Angry Dolphins) really outdid themselves with the cup decorations.

I seriously think we need to start the IRCPQ Brewery with bottles and cans that look like those. Thanks to all the teams that have done their part to make a piece of coffee house detritus into something we can call our own. You guys are the best!

Some of you are the best, anyway. Best for the night were the members of the Angry Dolphins and best for the month were the members of Seaman Cumming Round the Mountain. Everyone else is at most second best. Don't fret too much though, because we'll reset all the scores next week and for a brief moment you'll all be the best again.

Also, we'll all have the chance to order Seaman Cumming Round the Mountain cocktails next Wednesday. Hooray cocktails! See you next week, and until then I hope Mr. Sandman brings you a dream with lots of wavy hair like Liberace.

Evil Mr. Phil
