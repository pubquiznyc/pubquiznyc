Weekly Recap for 05/01/2013

Last night's Queens Day celebration was fantastic. Our mugs were overflowing with Dutch beer, and the entire café had an orange glow to it. Maybe it was the orange t-shirts. Maybe it was the orange foam crowns. The beads? Maybe. But it certainly wasn't all that bizarre Dutch wood. We're still not sure what those blocks of wood are for, but if anyone figures it out we'd love to hear.

Dr. Jordan and I thought about going dutch and letting you, the audience, write questions while we sat around drinking and scratching our heads. That would have been a nice break. Sometimes writing all these questions is hard work. But, then we realized we could just write a bunch of half-assed questions about the Dutch and still avoid doing any real work.

Speaking of half-assed questions... Am I getting senile or isn't Flight of the Conchords required viewing in the IRCPQ curriculum? For those of you who are unfamiliar with "business socks", I give you FotC.

And, now you know why they call them business socks. This stuff is very important, guys. So is recycling.

The scores at the beginning of the night were a little soft, but by the end we had some pretty solid numbers posted. At the top of the list was 2,000 Pounds of Stampeding Revenge.

Evil Mr. Phil
