Weekly Recap for 03/28/2012

The month that comes in like a lion and out like a lamb makes me think we should have had more questions about William Blake poems. Unfortunately, we had none. Instead, we had pop culture, lots of beer, and another comeback. It's exciting to see one team surge in the last week to take home the championship cup.

Well, it's exciting for us, not for the team that lost the lead. Sorry Ice Road Truckers Cumming Across Seaman Lake.

On the Narragansett Beer night, we chugged their lager and gave away their tall boy can holders. Also, Narragansett offered twelve rebus puzzles. Every team that got all of the puzzles won a free lager. Congratulations to the 11 teams that earned a free beer.

So what were the rounds? That's what you want to know if you weren't there. Right, Trivialis? Bunny Slippers? University of Inwood? Plan C? We miss you all. Come on back.

The first round was all about magazines. If you followed the hint, you read all about the Spaniard who started Hola and its Hello! empire.

The second round kept it literary. Dr. Jordan named a movie and you had to write down the author of the book.

Round three was all about words that end in "tch." It helped to know about Romney's staffer's boneheaded comment comparing their campaign to an Etch-a-Sketch.

Round four was about math and myth, where it helped to remember PEMDAS and what Persephone ate in Hades.

Round five was all about Franks. Did you know Anne Frank was born in Frankfort?

The common bond worked like this. There were nine questions where the questions and answers were seemingly unrelated, but really they all described one type of market.

Many teams did well, but one team ended the night a bit better than all the others. For the first time in three weeks, we did not have a three question tie-breaker.

Congratulations to The Blockheaded Bracegirdles who, somewhat suspiciously, dropped their dead weight and adopted a few Bunny Slippers. They won for the night and for the month. We look forward to their punch next week and to seeing photos of their antics with the championship cup.

We look forward to your new team names for April.
