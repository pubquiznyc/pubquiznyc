Weekly Recap for 08/28/2013

Oh what a disastrous quiz night. Few things went well. Most things went completely wrong.

OJ forgot to give answer sheets to most of the teams, Dr. Jordan cruelly tricked some perfectly ernest chuggers, The Quizmasters' Cup was returned late and in rough shape, and I both forgot to announce team names and spoiled the endings of ten great movies. A cyborg even showed up with some new-fangled, computerized glasses. A night of total ruin, indeed.

What I'd call a perfect quiz.

Luckily we had a room full of players that wouldn't be deterred in their quest for fun and prizes. High scores were posted by all, on topics ranging from board games to Manhattan neighborhoods and from saints to mayors of New York. It was a close night that wasn't decided until the very end when Woman and Husband finished in sole possession of the nightly prize. But, that wasn't all. With August coming to an end, we your quizmasters did some extra math to determine the winners of our monthly prize. It looked like we might have a new team in the winner's circle, but at the last moment Bunny Slippers Make My Nipples Tingle made our score sheets tingle. They won the All Shows tickets, a cocktail named in their honor, and a month with The Cup. We hope they treat it well. Sorry to Duke Silver, and sorry to the Tammies. We hope to see you guys next month.

And now, a racially insensitive Google image search for you to try out, "Zwarte Piet".

Evil Mr. Phil
