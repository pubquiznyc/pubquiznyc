Weekly Recap for 09/26/2012

The guys from the Bronx Brewery were in the house again on Wednesday sharing their new Rye Pale Ale with us. If the delicious beer wasn't enough, they also brought a bunch of t-shirts, pint glasses, and stickers. What could be better?

As for trivia, Dr. Jordan delivered another movie quotes round, a 9th-grade history round, and a round on famous covers. I had doctors and founding dates by request, and the common bond was all about the Simpsons. Sounds like a pretty good spread based on the topics, but in fact over half the questions during the night were about US Vice Presidents. We promise to steer clear of the US history for next week.

At the end of the night, the University of Inwood had won out for the night, which earned them the top score for September. They walked away with the gift certificate, the All Shows tickets, the promise of a cocktail next week, and they regained possession of the Quizmasters' Cup. We hope to see a lot of pictures on the Cup's facebook page.

Also, a very happy birthday to Ian, and thanks for sharing your cake! Everyone enjoy your weekend. See you on Wednesday!

Evil Mr. Phil
