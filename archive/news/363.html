Weekly Recap for 07/11/2012 (Horrible Things From New Jersey Edition)

As I sit here high atop my Manhattan apartment building, my gaze is ever westward. Westward into the park, westward across the water, westward to the cliffs - the cliffs of New Jersey. As I look at those cliffs, I can't help but wonder what horrible things from New Jersey will be arriving today. But, I'm comforted by the fact that none of it could ever be as horrible as Wednesday's horrible things from New Jersey...

But enough about horrible things from New Jersey. At this week's pub quiz we saw the return of estranged quizmaster and New Jersey native, Dr. Jordan, as a member of the Youth In Asia. And, speaking of members, the true horror of the evening was when first-time quizmaster That Rachel asked her pint on us question. Little did we know that lurking on the far side of the Hudson was a urologist/collector that had a suitcase in his possession containing the "maltreated strip of buckskin shoelace" known as Napoleon's Penis! Even less did we know that before That Rachel could even finish asking her question about said "shriveled eel", someone from University of Inwood (Jen I think) would be throwing her arms into the air and shouting "Napoleon's Penis" at the top of her lungs in a quiet bar full of people.

All that and we had rounds of trivia questions, too! There was wordplay, baby animals, mini common bonds, famous birthplaces, high-flying acrobatics, derring-do, and hijinks galore. Unfortunately, we also had to bid farewell to the T-Birds (Brian and Caitlyn) who have departed for some modern place calling itself the new Mexico. They've been regulars at the quiz for a while, and we'll miss them. As for the quiz itself, That Rachel took control of the room in her first foray into quizmastering, continuing a steady trend of people making our jobs look easy. By the end of the night we had some incredibly tight scores, but the outright winner was "The Big Game: Pughkeepsie Mudhens vs. University of Inwood".

In Other News

As part of my ongoing quest to find more trivia, I've taken a job with noted purveyors of accumulated human knowledge, Google. I start on Monday, and to help me start off with a clean slate, I'll be at the Kilt tonight drinking a coma-inducing amount of Guinness and Red Breast. Everyone's invited to come on down and celebrate - I'll probably show up between 7 and 8.

See you next week! (Or tonight if you come out to drink.)

Evil Mr. Phil
