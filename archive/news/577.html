Weekly Recap for 11/13/2013

This week at The IRCPQ, just like ever other week, was special and unique. We were sponsored by Bread and Yoga, who brought some pretty fantastic shirts, bags, and free yoga classes. Marcela and Meghan of B&Y also helped out by running the chug-offs, distributing prizes, and demonstrating yoga positions.

As for trivia, I was joined in hosting by the pre-cognizant Mary O'Shag who concocted a round on local Inwood businesses long before we knew Bread and Yoga was sponsoring. We also had a round on folded things, an UGH round, and everything you ever wanted to know about balls. We "rounded" out the night with an inscrutable common bond on cellphone games. Congrats to the few team that got it. For everyone else, you've probably been spending your time better than the rest of us and avoiding time-sucking games.

Finally, it was Here Comes the Trivialis winning for the night by several lengths, moving them into the lead for the month of November. Speaking of November, I'd like to remind you that there WILL indeed be a quiz on the 27th. Many teams are severely depleted on the day before Thanksgiving, which could leave the door open for perseverant teams to steal the lead. Just you think about that!

Evil Mr. Phil
