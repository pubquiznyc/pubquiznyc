Weekly Recap for 11/07/2012

A pub quiz in a hurricane. A pub quiz in a blizzard. What's going to happen next week? A pub quiz while an old lady cuts cocaine in her dining room?

What promised to be a slow night during the freakish midweek blizzard turned into yet another night of raucous fun and great prizes. No Nickname Naomi showed up in a pinch to cover for Dr. Jordan who is still recovering from electionitis*, and as always she kicked ass. There were rounds on bests and worsts, double names, state quarters, watergate, and of course beer. There was a 10-way chug-off. And, there was almost a fight over the common bond at the end of the night, but fortunately Naomi had had plenty of wine and was well equipped to deal with it.

In the end it was The Hypno-Toads winning by a large margin, perhaps everyone else just couldn't take their eyes off them. See you next week when we find out what meteorological oddity awaits us next.

* - Symptoms include elevated levels of political facebook posts and a lack of interest in pub trivia.
