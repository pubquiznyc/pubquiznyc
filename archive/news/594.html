Weekly Recap for 01/01/2014 (Best Recap of the Year)

We had a pub quiz at Indian Road Café on New Year's Day. You won't believe what happened next!

Then again, maybe you will. What do I know? For one thing, we made a New Years Resolution to not phone it in this year. Then we immediately broke the resolution by doing some tired rounds on "new" stuff, "year" stuff, and things from 2013. If that wasn't predictable enough, we also had some uninspired pint on us questions. I'd like to say the common bond was phoned in as well, but I have to admit that an entire round on "butter" is pretty fantastic. Even if the trivia was obvious, at least you guys weren't too hung over from the night before to partake in some free Genesee.

Anyway, this Wednesday was the beginning of a new month, and we had a lot of new team names. However, it was the stalwart Woman and Husband leading the way at halftime. Though, by the end of the night, Elopements and Deployments had run off into the night to embark on adventures with their prize money. We hope they don't spend it all in one place. Actually... I guess they have to spend it all in one place since it's in the form of a gift certificate. Congrats guys, and good luck adventuring.

Oh, and near the end of the night, I apparently made a resolution to stop shaving. We'll have to see where that leads us. Anyway, I'm going to go to the other room and not shave. See you next week!

Evil Mr. Phil
